The problem

Your most skilled people are spending their days on paperwork.

Every renewal brings the same routine: chasing quotes from a panel of insurers, comparing them, keying details into portal after portal, writing the summary, binding, then sending documents to the client.

It has to be done, and done well. It just doesn't have to be done by the people you hired for their judgement and their client relationships.

Admin that has to be done — but doesn't need a broker to do it.

How it works

From renewal report to bound policy, without your brokers touching the keyboard.

You keep working the way you do today. We plug into the reports you already have.

You send the renewals

Upload your pending renewal report from your broking system, or have it arrive by email on a schedule. That's the whole set-up.

We start early

Each renewal is picked up automatically at the start date you choose — for example 50 days out — and given to a named assistant.

You make the call

Quotes from your insurer panel come back in one clear summary. Choose with a single click, or ask a question through the built-in notes.

We finish the job

We bind your choice and send you the client's policy documents in an email that's already written — just forward it.

Every step is time-stamped on the renewal's timeline, so you can always see what has happened and when.

Questions

What brokers usually ask.

Who actually does the work?

A dedicated admin team, supported by smart digital workers. Each renewal is given to a named assistant, who works it through to the end following your instructions for that product.

Do we lose control of our clients or our decisions?

No. You choose the insurer, and nothing is bound until you do. Your assistant asks questions through notes, and every action is recorded on the renewal's timeline.

What do we need to get started?

Your pending renewal report from your broking system, and a client report for contact details. There is no software to install and no integration to build.

Which broking systems does it work with?

It is built around the reports exported from INSIGHT today. If you use a different system, talk to us about what it can export.

Which insurers and products are covered?

We work to the insurer panel and products agreed with you, and follow your own instructions for each product. Products that aren't part of your agreement are left alone.

What happens to our client data?

Client details are used only to work the renewal. We'll go through how information is handled, stored and protected when we talk.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is straightforward and agreed up front, so you know the cost before you begin. Get in touch and we'll go through it with you.
